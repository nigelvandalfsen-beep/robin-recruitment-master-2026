How to Read This Waterfall Graph (Step-by-Step Logic)

From starting January 1 baseline to net realized headcount and plan bridge

Formula Cheatsheet
STEP 1
Start of Year (FTE)

The starting anchor of the chart. Corporate permanent headcount baseline recorded on January 1st (editable parameter, e.g. 209 FTE).

Anchor = Baseline
STEP 2 (ADD)
+ Filled positions

Every position that has been filled is added to the starting baseline. In granular view, this stacks NEW employees, contractors, interns, PhDs, and RPL roles.

Baseline + All Filled Roles
STEP 3 (DEDUCT)
Deductions until Current FTE

Deduct churn to isolate real permanent net gain:

  • Replacements: Replaces departing FTEs (turnover churn + PhDs).
  • Filled Contractors: Non-payroll interim staff.
  • Interns: Temporary student stages.
  • Filled Internal: Reassigning internal staff creates an empty backfill behind them.
  • Open RPL positions: Active replacement vacancies currently vacant.
− (Replacements + Filled Contractors + Interns + Filled Internal + Open RPL)
STEP 4 (RESULT)
Current FTE & Gap to Plan

The resulting Current FTE represents active net company headcount. The upward lavender bar shows the Gap to Plan (open requisitions needed to reach targeted plan).

The striped hatch section inside Gap to Plan highlights the open RPL positions accounted for in the deduction!

Current FTE + Gap = Target Plan
Summary Formula Current FTE = Start FTE + Filled Positions − (Replacements + Filled Contractors + Interns + Filled Internal + Open RPL positions)
